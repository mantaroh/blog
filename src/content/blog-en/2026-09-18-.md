For about two years now, I’ve been experimenting with how much of my everyday life I can preserve as data.

What is commonly referred to as a “life log.”

I’m not at the stage of trying to create any specific service yet. For now, I’m collecting data first and thinking about what I might be able to do with it afterward.

Currently, I’m mainly collecting the following kinds of data.

## What I’m collecting from Rabbit r1

I carry a Rabbit r1 with me to record logs of my everyday life.

- Photos taken every few minutes
  - Front-facing camera
  - Rear-facing camera
- Continuously recorded audio
- Location data from GPS

With this, I can look back, to some extent, on

“when and where I was, what I was looking at, and what kinds of conversations were happening around me.”

I’ve currently been accumulating this data for nearly two months.

https://x.com/_mantaroh_/status/2089548204144746714/photo/1



Rabbit r1 is an old device, but it brings together the basic functions of a camera, microphone, GPS, screen, and LTE (4G) connectivity, so I’m quite grateful for its existence. Also, since AOSP can run on it, installing a custom ROM and being able to run apps however I like is another one of its advantages.

https://github.com/mantaroh/rabbit-r1-recorder

## What I was doing on my PC

I also run a dedicated app on my desktop that saves a screenshot every minute.

As a result, information such as the following is also preserved chronologically:

- Which apps I had open
- Which websites I was viewing
- Which projects I was working on
- What kinds of documents I was creating

In other words, I’m recording my activities on my PC along with logs from the physical world.

## Communicating with my family

The origin of this logging system was a bot from around the time ChatGPT first appeared.

I have a system where messages with my wife go through a bot, and I’ve been saving those messages for nearly three years.

Not only the text, but also all the images exchanged are preserved.

Things like discussing trips, shopping, and our children.

This everyday communication has also become a fairly long-term dataset.

As part of revamping the bot’s operation, I’ve vectorized this data as well, making it searchable.

## Turning my children’s chores into data

For my children, I’ve created an app for “chore points.”

For example, records such as:

- Put away the dishes
- Cleaned
- Earned ○ points

These are also continuously saved as data.

## Avoiding vendor lock-in in everything

What I was particular about in this project was avoiding vendor lock-in.

Apple products are also excellent, and it seems that, recently, the Apple Watch has finally started summarizing recordings for you.

https://www.gizmodo.jp/article/apple-watch_series-12_siri_recap_health/

It’s an excellent product, and I’d like to try using it, but I’m concerned that integrating with Apple would introduce lock-in due to privacy and security requirements.

In particular, the cameras and screenshots I’m using this time are directly related to privacy, so I felt that ideally everything should be completed within my own environment. That’s why I chose to build everything myself. (Although, having said that, I’m making extensive use of OSS...)

I’m also being careful not to become locked into a vendor for the LLM at the core of the AI.

## What to do with the data collected?

When I organize it this way again,

- Photos
- Audio
- Location data
- My PC screen
- Messages with my family
- Photos with my family
- Records of my children’s activities

quite a lot of different aspects of my life have become data.

Until now, these were systems built separately for different purposes.

However, since I recently started recording life logs with Rabbit r1,

**What would become possible if I connected all of this along a timeline?**

One answer might be that I could search a broad range of things using my own data.

It would also be interesting if AI could discover things such as:

“I’ve been repeating the same task over and over lately.”

“This task seems like it could be automated.”

“I’ve looked into a similar problem before.”

## Maybe I want to build my own personal search engine

Perhaps my motivation was that, since I don’t have a particularly strong working memory, I wanted a search engine just for myself if possible.

The things I’ve seen, heard, talked about, and worked on, as well as my conversations with my family.

I want to make all of that searchable.

Another reason may be that current AI processing is still in a transitional phase, and I feel that if I wait until next year or the year after, the ways of expressing and using the data I have now may expand.

However, as I’ve continued collecting logs, I’ve gradually started to see “data that might be useful.”

For now, while continuing to accumulate a little more,

**I’m going to experiment with how much I can get AI to understand about my past.**
