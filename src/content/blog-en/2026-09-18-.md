For about two years, I have been experimenting with how much of my everyday life I can preserve as data.

The so-called “life log.”

I am not yet trying to create any specific service. I am still at the stage of collecting data first and thinking about what I can do with it afterward.

Currently, I am mainly collecting the following kinds of data.

## What I collect from Rabbit r1

I carry a Rabbit r1 with me to record logs of my everyday life.

- Photos taken every few minutes
  - Front-facing camera
  - Rear-facing camera
- Continuously recorded audio
- Location data from GPS

With this, I can look back to some extent on:

“When and where I was, what I was looking at, and what kinds of conversations were taking place around me.”

I have now been accumulating this data for nearly two months.

[https://x.com/*mantaroh*/status/2089548204144746714/photo/1](https://x.com/_mantaroh_/status/2089548204144746714/photo/1)



The Rabbit r1 is an old device, but I am very grateful that it brings together the basic functions of a camera, microphone, GPS, screen, and LTE (4G) connectivity. Another advantage is that since it runs AOSP, I can install a custom ROM and run apps however I want.

[https://github.com/mantaroh/rabbit-r1-recorder](https://github.com/mantaroh/rabbit-r1-recorder)

## What I was doing on my PC

On the desktop side, I also run a dedicated app that saves a screenshot every minute.

This means information such as the following is also preserved chronologically:

- Which apps I had open
- Which websites I was viewing
- Which project I was working on
- What kind of materials I was creating

In this way, I am recording not only logs of the physical world, but also my activities on my PC.

## Communicating with my family

The origin of this logging was a bot from around the time ChatGPT first came out.

I set things up so that my communications with my wife go through the bot, and I have been saving nearly three years’ worth of messages.

I save not only the text, but also all the images we exchanged.

Discussions about trips, shopping, and the children.

These everyday communications have also become data spanning a fairly long period of time.

As I revamp the bot’s operation, I am also vectorizing this data so that it can be searched.

## Turning the children's chores into data

For my children, I created an app for “chore points.”

For example, records such as:

- Put away the dishes
- Cleaned
- Earned ○ points

These are also continuously saved as data.

## Avoiding vendor lock-in in everything

One thing I was particular about in building this system was avoiding vendor lock-in.

Apple products are also excellent, and recently it seems that the Apple Watch has finally started compiling recordings for you.

[https://www.gizmodo.jp/article/apple-watch_series-12_siri_recap_health/](https://www.gizmodo.jp/article/apple-watch_series-12_siri_recap_health/)

The product seems excellent and I would like to try it, but I am concerned that integrating with Apple would impose restrictions due to privacy and security requirements.

In particular, the cameras and screenshots I am using this time are directly connected to privacy, so I thought it would be ideal to keep everything within my own environment, and chose to build everything myself. (Though I am saying that while making extensive use of OSS...)

I am also careful to avoid vendor lock-in with the LLM at the core of the AI.

## What should I do with the data I've collected?

When I organize it this way again, quite a lot of different parts of my life have become data:

- Photos
- Audio
- Location data
- My PC screen
- Messages with my family
- Photos with my family
- Records of my children's activities

Until now, these were systems built separately for different purposes.

However, since I recently started recording a life log with the Rabbit r1,

**I wonder what I could do if I connected all of this along a timeline.**

One answer might be that I could search my own data across a broad range of areas.

Beyond just searching, it would be interesting if AI could identify things such as:

“I have been repeating the same task many times recently.”

“This task looks like it could be automated.”

“I have researched a similar problem before.”

Perhaps I want to build a search engine just for myself

Since I do not have a particularly strong working memory, perhaps my motivation was that I wanted a search engine dedicated to me, if possible.

The things I have seen, heard, and talked about; the work I have done; and my interactions with my family.

To make all of those searchable.

Another reason may be that the current state of AI processing is still transitional, so if I look forward to next year or the year after that, the ways in which my current data can be used may expand.

However, as I have actually continued collecting logs, I have gradually started to see data that “might be useful.”

For now, while accumulating a little more,

**I am going to experiment with how much I can get AI to understand about my past.**
