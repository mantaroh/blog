I came down with the flu.

My eldest son had been coughing ever since he came home from club activities on Saturday, and the next day, my coughing became unstoppable too.

Sunday night, it finally kicked in.

I started thinking about treatment for the flu + AI.



### Asking About Treatment

This was the thing I least wanted to do because it involves medical care, but when I looked online, every UI seemed to have an AI chat attached to it, so I ended up looking at the answers.

They were mostly harmless answers, more or less like this:

- Consult a medical institution if your fever or cough continues.
- Don't take medicine based on your own judgment.
- Drink plenty of fluids and rest your body.

Well, obviously. No new information.

Since I was going to sleep, I decided to let only the mini PC's OS setup run and go to bed.



### Asking What to Eat

But humans get hungry.

My wife was at work and the kids were at school today, so I was alone at home (+ the rabbit), which meant I had to prepare my own food.

But my body felt sluggish, and making something was a pain. So when I wondered what to do, I asked AI:

“I have a cold and feel sluggish. What should I eat?”

- Choose foods that are easy to digest.
- If you have no appetite, don't push yourself and get some rest.
- Drink as much fluid as possible.

...

There was some gomoku rice and a Nichirei plate of miso eggplant, so I ate that.



### Power Outage Due to the Apartment Building's Regular Inspection

I prepared tethering and got a mobile battery ready.

I thought, “Now even if the power goes out, I can read while running a fan.” But then I felt like I had to use AI here too, so I asked it to suggest ways to spend time during a power outage.

- Since the environment is different from usual, try starting something new.
- Be careful that there isn't anything that could cause a fire when the power comes back on.
- I think it would be a good idea to go outside, even if only during that time period.

...

I have a cold, though.



### The AI I Took Along to Pick Up My Wife

I took Rabbit r1, which is always recording and photographing my conversations and photos with my wife, along to pick her up.

I somehow managed to drive, so I got to my wife's workplace without any problems!

Below is the content of a conversation between my wife and me that was recorded.

`Wife: Don't you think the car got clean? I wiped it with a towel at the gas station, and it got really clean.`

`Mitsuru: I thought so. Especially the mats.`

`Wife: I didn't do the mats.`

`Mitsuru: You took them out, then.`

It had recorded even the most ordinary parts of our daily life, and the thought of our household AI making use of this was unbearable.



### Something Strange When I Got Home

After picking her up and returning home, I sat down at my desk and noticed something felt off.

The mini PC's LED was blinking in an impossible-looking way, begging for help.

When I started it up, it seemed to have stopped at the UEFI screen. Without really knowing why, I selected the disk in UEFI, but there was nothing on it...

Could it have been something around the bootloader when the power went out?

I'm going to bed.
